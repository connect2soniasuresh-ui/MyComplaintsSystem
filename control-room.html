Scenarios and action plans

Three scenarios set by the metric bands. The one that matches this month is outlined. Each metric's own fix is in the last column of Metric definitions.

Best case

How we know

At least 70% of metrics in their best band, none in the worst, no guardrail breached.

What it looks like

ARR of ₹12 lakh or more, 75% or more of complaints resolved within 24 hours, half of pilots converting, and officers using the console every week.

Action plan

  1. Move new banks to the full Professional tier price and close the founding-bank offer.
  2. Open a second state federation and start a regional rural bank pilot.
  3. Hire a second customer success person before the number of banks outgrows one.
  4. Build voice lodging in regional languages for customers who don't type.
  5. Keep the guardrails: the monthly confidential-data audit and reveal-log review continue.

Owner and review: PM with the GTM lead; monthly.

Average case This month

How we know

Neither the best nor the worst rule applies.

What it looks like

Growing, but some metrics stall: 60–75% within 24 hours, payments slow to arrive, and one or two control metrics drifting the wrong way.

Action plan

  1. Take the two weakest metrics and run their fix from Metric definitions for 30 days.
  2. Fortnightly review with each bank's Principal Nodal Officer, using the bank table.
  3. Push online lodging: QR posters in branches and tracking links in SMS.
  4. Move new contracts to annual billing in advance to bring collection time down.
  5. Hold new pilots until pilot-to-paid conversion is back at 50%.

Owner and review: PM with the CX lead; fortnightly.

Worst case

How we know

A guardrail is breached (confidential details stored, or a complaint open past RBI's 30 days), or 4 or more metrics are in the worst band.

What it looks like

A bank is exposed to RBI or losing trust in the product, revenue stalls and the first cancellations appear.

Action plan

  1. Incident response within 24 hours for any guardrail breach: fix it, tell the bank in writing, write a root-cause note.
  2. Freeze new sales and onboarding until the breach is closed.
  3. Founder call to every bank this week, then a weekly written update until back to Average.
  4. Switch AI routing to rules only if AI errors or overrides are part of the cause.
  5. Cut events and ads to protect cash, and re-check the target segment.

Owner and review: PM and TECH lead with the whole team; daily until out.
