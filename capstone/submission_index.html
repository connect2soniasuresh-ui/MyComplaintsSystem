Two-minute walkthrough of the product

  1. Open connect2soniasuresh-ui.github.io/MyComplaintsSystem/?demo.
  2. Lodge: pick a service and issue (or “Something else (not listed)” and type a short issue), describe the problem and submit. Type OTP 123456 in the description first: it is flagged and blocks submission. You get a reference number, the 24-hour resolution target and the Day 30 final-reply date.
  3. Track: click “Track this complaint” to see its status and history.
  4. Staff: open the Staff tab and sign in with any email and password (demo only). See the queue with its 24-hour clock, routing team and escalation level. Open a case, show contact details (the view is logged), re-route it and send an update. A message asking for an OTP is blocked.
  5. Monthly report: from the staff queue, open “Monthly report”: received, resolved, % within 24 hours and 30 days, by service and team. Download it as CSV.

The AI complaint assistant (chat) runs on the live site without ?demo; demo mode hides it because it needs the server-side AI function.
